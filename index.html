I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the Vancouver School of Economics, University of British Columbia. My research interests lie in the areas of environmental economics and public finance.

I am also a 2025–2027 Ph.D. Fellow at the Stone Centre on Wealth and Income Inequality at UBC.

My job market paper studies who bears the burden of carbon taxes, using the introduction of British Columbia’s carbon tax as a setting to study incidence on workers and firm owners.
